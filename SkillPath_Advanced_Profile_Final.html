Problem Statement & Solution

Problem Statement ID: 26135

❌ Existing Problem


  • Employment outcomes are difficult to track.
  • Skill gaps may remain unidentified.
  • Training data may focus mainly on enrollment and certification.
  • Job retention and wage progression may be incomplete.
  • Students may not know which career is right for them.

✅ Our Solution


  • Create a digital student skill profile.
  • Identify skill gaps.
  • Recommend suitable careers.
  • Provide personalized learning roadmaps.
  • Track progress from training to employment.
